Make the budget fit your life. The 50/30/20 split is a guideline. Essential bills can exceed 50%. Count minimum required debt payments under Needs; extra debt payments and savings belong in the 20% bucket. Money left to assign is not automatically safe to invest: first account for all upcoming bills and commitments.
How do I come back and update it?

Use the arrows above to switch months. Each month saves separately in this browser. New months copy expense names, targets and income, but Current entries start empty. Tap ⋯ to copy a plan again or reset just this month. Before switching devices or clearing browser data, select Back up all months, then use Restore backup on the new device. PDF and image exports show only the selected month.
